## Overview

The CMMC 2.0 Evidence Tracker is a purpose‑built Notion template that turns compliance chaos into a calm, auditable system. Map NIST SP 800‑171 controls, track evidence and gaps, generate assessor‑friendly views, and keep your team on the same page.

- Designed for OSCs heading toward CMMC L2
- Built by a security leader with 20+ years of enterprise and federal compliance experience
- Local‑first friendly and tool‑agnostic

## What you get

- **CMMC Controls Hub**
    - All 110 controls with fields for requirement text, assessment objectives, status, owner, and priority
- **Evidence Register**
    - Structured log of artifacts with source, location, control mapping, reviewer, collection cadence, and validation status
- **SSP and POA&M Trackers**
    - Light, practical pages and linked databases to draft SSP sections and manage remediation work
- **Readiness Dashboards**
    - Executive roll‑ups, control heatmaps, overdue evidence, and assessor‑ready views
- **Workflow Templates**
    - To‑dos, review cycles, collection checklists, and meeting notes
- **Starter Data**
    - Example control mappings and evidence examples to show best practices

## FAQs

### Does this include the official control texts?

Includes structured placeholders and fields aligned to NIST SP 800‑171 and CMMC assessment objectives, with links for you to add your licensed or public copies.

### Can this replace my GRC tool?

For many small and mid‑size OSCs, yes. If you already have a GRC, use this as your operations hub and export artifacts to your system of record.

### How do I migrate my spreadsheets?

Use the included CSV import templates. Most users migrate evidence in under a day.
